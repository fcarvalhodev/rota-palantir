Palantir Route

What does Palantir have to do with war?

Palantir is an American software company that works for armies, police forces and spies. Here is what it does, who it works for, what is known about Gaza, the role of other big tech companies, the bridge to Trump and what it did in Brazil. Every fact has a source and a label saying how much of it is proven.

📊 See the summary in icons🎮 Play: The Detective Board

The short answer

Palantir makes software that pulls scattered data together and helps decisions get made fast. It was born with money from a CIA fund and today is one of the largest technology suppliers to the Pentagon. It also works for US immigration police and, since January 2024, has a partnership with Israel's Ministry of Defence for "war-related missions". That is documented.

That its technology picks targets in Gaza is an accusation by a UN rapporteur, and the company denies it. Other big tech companies help militaries in a different way: with cloud and artificial intelligence, not by handing over your posts or music. And Palantir also worked under the Lula government: the education fund, run by a PT appointee, used its software until July 2026.

What Palantir does, in one picture

Think of a detective's board in a film: photos, maps and papers pinned to the wall, linked by string. Palantir builds that board on a computer, with millions of pieces of information, and links them on its own.

An important detail: Palantir does not have its own database of people's lives. It sells the software, and the client puts in the data it already has. So what the software does depends on who uses it: it can plan school meals or pick a target. Critics say putting everything in one place makes surveillance and abuse easier. Documented

Where it came from

2003

Founded by Peter Thiel, Alex Karp and three other partners. Thiel is the billionaire who created PayPal. Karp has been the company's chief executive since 2004. The name comes from the magic stones in "The Lord of the Rings" that let you see far away. Documented

CIA

Seed money from a CIA fund. In-Q-Tel, the investment fund linked to the American spy agency, put in about US$ 2 million. Thiel paid for most of the first US$ 30 million. Documented

2020

Goes public. In 2025, it made US$ 4.48 billion in revenue. In 2024, more than half of sales, 53.7%, went to governments. Its market value passed US$ 400 billion in 2025. Documented

What about the other big tech companies?

They help militaries in a different way: renting out cloud (giant computers) and artificial intelligence models. In the sources we read, none of them directly hands Palantir users' posts, photos or music. But Meta's and xAI's AIs were trained on users' posts, and there are other indirect paths, explained just below.

Microsoft

  • According to The Guardian, Israel's Unit 8200 spy unit stored surveillance data on Palestinians' phone calls on the Azure cloud.
  • In September 2025, after an internal review, Microsoft cut off those services to the unit. Documented

  • Project Nimbus: a US$ 1.2 billion cloud contract with the Israeli government, from 2021.

The indirect path of your data

Palantir does not buy data from social networks. But what you post reaches militaries and governments, which are Palantir's biggest clients, by more than one path. Each step has its own level of proof:

1

Your posts train the AI that goes to militaries. Meta trained Llama on public Facebook and Instagram posts, including Brazilians', and X trains Grok on users' posts. Then Llama was opened to US defence, with Palantir among the partners, and Grok joined Palantir's platform. The AI does not carry a file with your profile, but it carries what it learned from millions of profiles. Which version is used and whether it includes Brazilian data is not known. Documented

2

Big tech hands data to the government. Google, Apple and Meta handed over data from more than 3.5 million accounts to US authorities from 2014 to 2025 through official requests; counting espionage requests under the FISA law, it is 6.9 million, according to a survey by privacy company Proton. In February 2026, according to The New York Times, the Department of Homeland Security sent hundreds of subpoenas to Google, Meta, Reddit and Discord seeking the identity of anonymous accounts that criticised ICE, and some were complied with. In 2013, Edward Snowden's documents revealed PRISM, an NSA programme that collected data from large internet companies; all of them denied giving direct server access. Documented

3

The government also buys data from middlemen. ICE and the border police buy phone location data, collected from apps, from intermediary companies, without a warrant. More than 65 Democratic lawmakers asked for an investigation. The sellers are not Meta or Google but smaller intermediaries. Documented

4

Does this data go into Palantir's software? ICE has been a Palantir client since 2013 and uses ImmigrationOS to decide who to deport. Data obtained from social networks or bought may end up analysed in a Palantir system, but no public source confirms a concrete case. Open

And direct sales from Meta, Google or Apple to Palantir? They do not appear in any source. Not shown

The bridge to Trump, Vance and Flávio

There is a link in three steps. Each step has a different level of proof.

1

Shared ideas. In the book The Technological Republic, from 2025, Karp argues that tech companies should go back to working with the government for national security and for the West. Trump's National Security Strategy, from December 2025, makes the Americas a priority and seeks to keep out the influence of outside powers such as China. Flávio Bolsonaro publicly backs this alignment with the US. Documented

2

Network of people. Thiel, Palantir's co-founder, hired JD Vance at his fund, introduced Vance to Trump in 2021 and gave US$ 15 million to his 2022 Senate campaign. Vance is now vice-president and was at the meeting with Flávio at the White House in May 2026. Former Palantir employees worked at Elon Musk's DOGE. Documented

3

Action in Brazil's election. No source shows a contract, money, data or meeting between Palantir and Flávio's campaign, or Lula's. Being in the same network shows similar interests; it does not prove the company acted in the election. Not shown

Palantir in Brazil

2023 – Jul 2026

Education fund (FNDE), under the Lula government

FNDE, the Education Ministry fund that pays for school meals, school transport and student loans, used Palantir's Foundry and AIP software. Palantir was not chosen in a process of its own: it came in through Amazon's online marketplace, inside a cloud contract of up to R$ 21.66 million with Serpro, signed in November 2023 without a tender, as the law allows for federal state companies. Use began in March 2024, with school transport data. FNDE says it used only public data, hosted in Brazil, and dropped Palantir in July 2026.

Who decided: Serpro says choosing Palantir was "exclusively FNDE's" decision. The fund was, and still is, headed by Fernanda Pacobahyba, a Ceará tax auditor appointed by then Education Minister Camilo Santana, of the PT, who is now in the Senate and on Lula's campaign. There is no record that the presidential palace or Lula himself decided on its use.

Who raised it: sociologist Sérgio Amadeu, in December 2025, and then Congresswoman Sâmia Bomfim, of PSOL, a party allied with the government, with 25 questions. There is no proof of wrongdoing or favouritism; what is being demanded is transparency.

Is it true?

True

“Palantir works for the Israeli army in the war.” Since January 2024 there has been a strategic partnership with Israel's Ministry of Defence for “war-related missions”, announced by the company itself.

Not shown

“Palantir built Lavender, the AI that picks targets in Gaza.” Lavender and Gospel are Israeli army systems. The UN rapporteur links Palantir to target selection; the company denies building or taking part in those two systems. So far, nobody has shown a document that settles it.

False

“Spotify sells your data for war.” What exists is the investment by the fund of Daniel Ek, Spotify's founder, in Helsing, a German drone and military AI company. There is no record of Spotify user data going to Helsing or Palantir.

Half true

“X and Facebook hand your posts to Palantir.” They do not hand over the posts. But Meta trained Llama on public Facebook and Instagram posts, and X trains Grok on users' posts, and both AIs were made available for military use and on Palantir's platform. The AI does not bring your profile, but it carries what it learned from it.

Half true

“Your social media data ends up in the hands of the US government.” Google, Apple and Meta handed over data from millions of accounts to US authorities through official requests, and the government also buys phone location data from intermediaries. But this depends on a request or a purchase, it is not everything about everyone, and it is not proven that this data goes into Palantir's systems.

True

“American big tech works for militaries.” Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta, Anthropic and xAI have cloud and AI contracts or partnerships with governments and militaries, in the US and in Israel. Microsoft cut off some services to Israel's Unit 8200 in 2025.

False

“Palantir only works for right-wing governments.” It grew with contracts from Obama, Biden and Trump. In Brazil, the Lula government's education fund, run by a PT appointee, used its software from 2024 until July 2026.

Not shown

“Palantir is helping elect Flávio Bolsonaro.” There are shared ideas and a network of people, from Thiel to Vance and Trump. But no source shows a contract, money, data or meeting between the company and any campaign in Brazil.

True

“Palantir was born with CIA money.” In-Q-Tel, a CIA-linked fund, invested about US$ 2 million at the start. Most of the money came from Peter Thiel.

Summary

  • Palantir makes software that pulls data together and helps decisions. It was born with money from a CIA fund and is one of the Pentagon's largest suppliers, with contracts from Democratic and Republican governments.
  • It has worked for Israel in the war since January 2024. That it picks targets in Gaza is a UN accusation, denied by the company.
  • Other big tech companies help militaries with cloud and AI. There is no proof they hand Palantir the data of social media users, but they do hand data to governments, which are its clients, and the Spotify case is different: its founder's investment in a drone company.
  • The bridge to Trump and Vance is real, through ideas and people. The bridge to Flávio's campaign has not been shown.
  • In Brazil, the Lula government's education fund used Palantir until July 2026. Serpro says the choice was FNDE's own, and who asked for the tool is not yet public.

Every fact carries the link to where it came from. Investigation and accusation are not conviction, and the company's version is in the text where it responded.

Sources and limits of this reading

US contract values are ceilings, that is, the most that can be spent, not what was paid. Military and intelligence contracts have secret parts, so no outsider knows everything Palantir does for Israel or the Pentagon. The UN rapporteur's report is an accusation, not a court ruling, and was rejected by the governments of Israel and the US. Palantir's letter is the company's version. Some company information comes from English Wikipedia, which gathers reporting; the core facts were checked against the original sources cited. On Brazil, the amount paid to Palantir alone by FNDE was not disclosed. The link to Trump and Vance is one of people and ideas; it does not prove action in Brazil's election.
